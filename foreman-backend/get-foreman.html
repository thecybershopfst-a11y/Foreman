Foreman

Your own copy of Foreman, fully separate from anyone else's.

Finance, invoicing, CRM, pipeline, tasks, inventory, and department bots run by a Supervisor — in one place. Click below and it deploys under your own account, with your own private data, in a few minutes.

  • Click the button — it takes you to Render (free to sign up, no card needed to start).
  • Render reviews what it's about to create (a web service + a database) and asks you to approve.
  • Wait a few minutes while it builds. Render shows you the progress.
  • You get a real web address — that's yours. Open it, and Foreman is ready to set up your business.
What's already set up for you: a real database so your data doesn't disappear, and a private access code generated automatically (find it under your new service's "Environment" tab on Render if you ever need it).

What's off by default: AI-drafted content (Marketing, the Supervisor, document generation) — turning that on means adding your own Anthropic API key under Environment, which is optional and has its own small cost per use. Everything else works fully without it.
