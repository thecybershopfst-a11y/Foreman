Before you buy

Questions people ask first

What if I lose the file?

We'll always re-send you a fresh copy of the app, free. Your actual business data lives only in your own browser, though — hang onto the daily backup file it saves to your Downloads folder, since that's what lets you restore everything into a new copy if something goes wrong.

Do updates cost extra?

Your purchase covers the current version. A future major version may be released separately — nothing recurring, ever, but not an automatic free upgrade either.

Is my data really local-only?

Yes. Foreman OS runs entirely in your browser with no server. AI features (Supervisor, Marketing drafts) only activate if you connect your own Claude account — nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to use them.

What are the automatic daily backups?

Once a day, on your first save, Foreman OS writes a real .json backup file to your device's Downloads folder automatically — so a cleared browser or a new device doesn't mean lost data. It's a local file, not a cloud backup.
