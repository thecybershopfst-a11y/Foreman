Before you buy

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You double-click the file, it opens in your browser, and you start typing into forms — the same as any app. Nothing to install, no command line.

What if I don't want to use the AI parts?

Skip them entirely. Finance, invoicing, CRM, pipeline, and inventory all run on plain code and work fully offline — Foreman only reaches out to Claude if you open the Marketing, Documents, or Supervisor tab and choose to.

Where does my business data actually go?

Nowhere but your own browser. It's stored locally on your device — not on our servers, because we don't have any. Back it up any time with the Export button, and use Import to move it to another computer.

What happens if I switch computers, or my browser data gets cleared?

Export your data first (Automations tab) and Import it on the new machine. It's a manual step today — worth doing on a regular schedule, the same way you'd back up anything that matters.

Is this a subscription?

No. $299 CAD once. No seat limits, no monthly bill from us, nothing that stops working if a card expires.
